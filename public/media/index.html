The opportunity

Financial services lead supply is broken three ways.

Buyers tell us the same story across every vertical we operate in. The supply side is fragmented, the compliance posture is shaky, and the quality is wildly inconsistent. CallReady was built to solve all three at once.

01 · FRAGMENTED
A dozen publishers per vertical.
Buyers manage a sprawl of supplier relationships, IOs, and integrations. Quality varies by source. No one is accountable for the whole funnel.
02 · NON-COMPLIANT
Consent trails are thin.
Affiliate networks pass leads with missing TrustedForm certs, missing LeadiD, or consent language that won't survive a TCPA challenge. Buyers absorb the risk.
03 · UNQUALIFIED
Volume without intent.
Incentivized traffic, co-reg, and email drops produce leads that don't pick up the phone. Contact rates collapse and CPA balloons.
CallReady solves all three. Owned. Compliant. Qualified.
